## How to sell your strategies on the MQL Market?

First, you will need to register on the MQL 5 Market which is described in the [next chapter](creating-mql4-mql-5-product-on-mql-market-step-by-step.md). As a developer you can sell your strategies there but you need to follow certain rules (the complete list of rules is available here).

As a developer, you need to take into account that once you sell your strategy MQL market automatically gets a fee of 20 % from every transaction.

The next step is you need to upload your strategy to the MQL Market, and submit it for their approval.  
When the strategy is approved, MQL 5 Market then adds extra protection. Every strategy sold at the Market is encrypted with a special key to protect the Product from illegal use.

The encryption key is unique for every Buyer and it is bound to his computer, so that all the Products in the Market have automatic copy protection.

Moreover, you need to set the number of strategy activations (the minimum number of activations is 5).  
Single activation means that your strategy is bound to the specific buyer’s hardware, so that the copy of the strategy cannot work on another PC.  
If the activation limit is exceeded, the buyer will have to purchase the product again. You can modify the number of strategy activation at any time.
