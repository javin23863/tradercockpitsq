If you want to switch the current installation without losing your work, just go to about and change your license, as you can see on the following screen.

[![](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/license-750x470.png)](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/license-750x470.png)

After applying the change, we recommend restarting SQX.
