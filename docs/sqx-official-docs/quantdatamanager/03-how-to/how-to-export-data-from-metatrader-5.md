# How to export data from Metatrader 5

- Source: <https://strategyquant.com/doc/quantdatamanager/how-to-export-data-from-metatrader-5/>
- Section: QuantDataManager › How to...
- Fetched: 2026-09-04

---

In this tutorial, we will show you how to export available data from Metatrader 5 to a CSV file.

**Open Metatrader 5 and the right click on the symbol in a market watch:**

[![](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/market_watch2.png)](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/market_watch2.png)

**Click on symbols**

[![](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/symbol.png)](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/symbol.png)

**Click on specific symbol**

[![](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/symbol_mt5.png)](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/symbol_mt5.png)

**Click on the bars tab**

[![](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/bars.png)](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/bars.png)

**Select the date range and click on request**

Please note that data download can take some time

[![](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/select_data.png)](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/select_data.png)

**Then you will see the bars and you have to select them**

[![](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/select_bars.png)](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/select_bars.png)

**Click on export**

[![](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/export_bars.png)](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/export_bars.png)

**Choose the folder for saving data**

[![](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/save.png)](https://strategyquant.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/save.png)

That’s it.

Please, note that available data in Metatrader could be short. It depends on what is your broker offering.
